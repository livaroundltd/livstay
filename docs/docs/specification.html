    LivStay

    An open protocol for personal agents to find, ask about and book independent homes.

    Draft 0.1 · 23 October 2026 · Extension livstay.org/stays, version "0" · Builds on Personal Agent Protocol (Poppy) draft 0.2 · Licensed under the Apache License 2.0

    Status: draft. This document is a working draft for implementers and reviewers. Until 1.0 a revision can break compatibility with earlier drafts. LivAround is the reference implementation; the protocol is not owned by it. Feedback: open an issue.

    1. Introduction

    Travellers are starting to plan and book stays through personal agents: assistants that act for one person. Today those agents mostly reach homes through online travel agencies (OTAs), which own the listing, the guest relationship and the price. Independent hosts and the platforms that serve them have no standard way to be found by an agent, answer its questions or take its booking directly.

    LivStay is that standard. It is an extension of Poppy: Poppy defines how a personal agent discovers a company, signs a user in, holds a session and talks to the company; LivStay defines what a stay is on top of that: the home, its facts, its availability, its terms, how an agent asks the host a question and how a booking is proposed, approved and confirmed.

    1.1 Goals

    • Recommendation before booking. An agent can tell a traveller why a home fits, from facts the host stands behind, before any booking call is made.
    • The traveller approves; the price is binding. No agent can commit a traveller's money to a price the traveller has not seen, and the price shown is the price charged.
    • The host's rules hold on every channel. An agent sees the same terms the host publishes everywhere else, and the host decides who can book instantly.
    • A human where it matters. An agent can put a question to the host, a person, before booking, and get an answer it can cite.
    • Start with what you have. A host platform can publish facts alone and add availability, enquiries and bookings later (§3.3).
    • Reuse, don't reinvent. Identity, sign-in, sessions, conversations and confirm-before-act are Poppy's. LivStay adds only what is specific to stays.

    1.2 Non-goals

    • Search ranking. LivStay does not say how agents rank homes.
    • Rate parity. LivStay takes no position on any contract between a host and an OTA.
    • Payment rails. LivStay does not define a payment protocol (§11).
    • Hotels. Nothing prevents a hotel from implementing LivStay, but the objects are shaped for homes rented by their owners or managers: one calendar, one host, house rules, a deposit.
    • Member-only exchange. Home swaps, points and circles are out of scope for this draft.

    1.3 Relationship to Poppy

    LivStay is a third-party Poppy extension, named under a domain its authors control, as Poppy section 7 requires. It depends on:

    Poppy feature Used for
    Discovery (/.well-known/poppy.json) Finding a host platform and its LivStay endpoint
    Authorization and Sessions Signed-out browsing; sign-in before holds and bookings
    Custom scopes The livstay:* permissions the traveller grants (§5)
    OpenAPI and MCP The LivStay endpoints and tools
    Conversations Enquiries to the host (§8) and booking updates (§12)
    operations extension Every booking, change and cancellation (§9, §10, §12)
    feedback Agents reporting stay tasks the Host Platform cannot do (§6.6)
    Web browsing (web.browser_session_endpoint) Identity checks and payment pages in the traveller's session (§9.5, §11)

    A host platform that implements LivStay MUST also implement Poppy draft 0.2 and the operations extension version "1". Where this document and Poppy disagree, Poppy governs everything Poppy defines.

    2. Conventions and terms

    The key words "MUST", "MUST NOT", "REQUIRED", "SHALL", "SHALL NOT", "SHOULD", "SHOULD NOT", "RECOMMENDED", "NOT RECOMMENDED", "MAY" and "OPTIONAL" in this document are to be interpreted as described in BCP 14 [RFC2119] [RFC8174] when, and only when, they appear in all capitals, as shown here.

    JSON examples are illustrative; field tables are normative. Amounts are decimal strings in the currency's major unit ("18500.00") with an ISO 4217 currency. Dates are ISO 8601 calendar dates in the home's local time zone; timestamps are RFC 3339 in UTC.

    Term Meaning
    Traveller The person the stay is for. Poppy's User.
    Personal Agent Software acting for the traveller. As in Poppy.
    Host The person or business that rents the home and answers for it.
    Host Platform The service that publishes homes under LivStay for one or more hosts. Poppy's Company. A host with their own site and a host platform serving thousands of hosts are both Host Platforms.
    Home A unit with one calendar that is booked as a whole. A room rented separately is its own Home.
    Stay A Home, a check-in date, a check-out date and a party.
    Facts Plain-language statements about a Home that the host stands behind (§6.2).
    Terms The rules a stay is sold under: price adjustments, cancellation, deposit, who may book instantly (§6.3).
    Quote A priced Stay, binding until it expires (§7.2).
    Booking operation A Poppy operations operation whose action is reserving a Stay (§9).
    Hold The period in which a proposed booking operation blocks the calendar (§9.2).
    Attestation A signed claim about a Home, Host or Traveller by any issuer (§13.2).
    Receipt A signed record of a write the Host Platform performed (§13.1).

    3. Overview

    3.1 The parties

     Traveller ──▶ Personal Agent ──Poppy session──▶ Host Platform ──▶ Host
                   │                                  │
                   │  poppy.json + livstay.org/stays  │  facts, calendar, terms,
                   │  enquiries (conversation)        │  answers to enquiries
                   │  bookings (operations)           │

    The Host Platform speaks for the Host. Conversations reach the Host as a person: the responder of an enquiry conversation is human (§8).

    3.2 A stay, in five steps

    1. Find. The agent reads poppy.json, then lists Homes and reads their Facts. No sign-in.
    2. Price. The agent asks for a Quote for exact dates and party. The Quote is binding until it expires.
    3. Ask (optional). The agent opens a conversation and puts the traveller's question to the Host.
    4. Book. The agent proposes a booking from the Quote. The Host Platform answers with a Poppy operation in state proposed, which holds the calendar. The agent shows the traveller the operation's summary; the traveller approves; the agent confirms.
    5. Stay. Updates, changes and cancellations arrive as operations and conversation messages. Every write returns a Receipt.

    3.3 Levels

    A Host Platform declares the highest level it supports. Each level includes the ones below.

    Level Name What an agent can do Requires
    0 Listed Read Homes, Facts, Terms, Protection §6
    1 Priced Read availability; get binding Quotes §7
    2 Reachable Ask the Host questions §8
    3 Bookable Book, change and cancel §9–§12

    Level 0 needs no sign-in and can be served from static files. A Host Platform SHOULD publish Level 0 before anything else: it is what lets an agent recommend a home.

    4. Advertising support

    4.1 Host Platform

    A Host Platform advertises LivStay in the extensions object of its poppy.json:

    {
  "protocol_version": "0.2",
  "organization": { "name": "LivAround", "domain": "stay.livaround.com" },
  "auth": {
    "issuer": "https://stay.livaround.com",
    "custom_scopes": {
      "livstay:enquire": "Ask hosts questions about a stay",
      "livstay:book": "Book stays you approve",
      "livstay:share_contact": "Give the host your phone number and email",
      "livstay:share_trip_context": "Tell the host the purpose of your trip and who is coming"
    }
  },
  "apis": [
    { "type": "openapi", "url": "https://stay.livaround.com/livstay/v0/openapi.json" },
    { "type": "mcp", "url": "https://stay.livaround.com/livstay/v0/mcp" }
  ],
  "extensions": {
    "operations": { "version": "1", "endpoint": "https://stay.livaround.com/poppy/operations" },
    "livstay.org/stays": {
      "version": "0",
      "endpoint": "https://stay.livaround.com/livstay/v0",
      "level": 3,
      "keys": "https://stay.livaround.com/.well-known/livstay-keys.json",
      "terms_of_service": "https://livaround.com/terms",
      "facts_site": "https://booking.livaround.com"
    }
  }
}

    Example 1. A Host Platform's poppy.json, LivStay parts only.

    Field Req. Description
    version Yes "0" for this draft. The extension's major version, as Poppy section 7 defines.
    endpoint Yes Base URL of the LivStay endpoints (§6–§10). HTTPS.
    level Yes Highest level supported, 0–3 (§3.3).
    keys Yes at level ≥1 A JWK Set [RFC7517] of the keys that sign Quotes, Receipts and Attestations.
    terms_of_service Yes at level 3 The Host Platform's terms the traveller books under.
    facts_site No Origin where each Home's Facts are also published as HTML (§6.5).

    A Host Platform at level 3 MUST list operations with version "1".

    4.2 Personal Agent

    A Personal Agent that supports LivStay lists it in its client metadata document:

    "extensions": {
  "operations": { "version": "1" },
  "livstay.org/stays": { "version": "0" }
}

    A Host Platform MUST NOT return a Quote, booking operation or LivStay conversation data to an agent that does not list livstay.org/stays. It MAY serve Level 0 data to any client, including clients with no Poppy session, because Facts are public by design (§6.5). A request that needs the extension from a client that does not list it fails with extension_required.

    5. Scopes

    LivStay uses Poppy's poppy:read and four custom scopes. Every write is a named scope the traveller sees at sign-in; a Host Platform MUST NOT require poppy:write for any LivStay action.

    Scope Grants Session
    poppy:read Homes, Facts, availability, Quotes; reading the traveller's own bookings once signed in Signed out or in
    livstay:enquire Opening enquiry conversations with Hosts (§8) Signed out or in
    livstay:book Proposing, confirming, changing and cancelling bookings (§9–§12) Signed in
    livstay:share_contact Releasing the traveller's direct phone and email to the Host (§12.3) Signed in
    livstay:share_trip_context Sending trip purpose, party and arrival notes to the Host (§9.4) Signed in

    Without livstay:share_contact, Host and traveller talk through the Host Platform's relay and neither sees the other's direct details.

    These scopes map onto the access scale Poppy describes for a hotel: search rooms is poppy:read; see reservations is poppy:read signed in; book a room is livstay:book. LivStay defines no "full access".

    6. The Home

    6.1 Listing Homes

    GET {endpoint}/homes

    Parameter Req. Description
    near No lat,lng
    radius_km No With near.
    area No A place name the Host Platform publishes (e.g. north-goa).
    check_in, check_out No If both are given, only Homes with that range available.
    guests No Party size.
    cursor No From a previous page.

    The response is { "homes": [Home summary], "next_cursor": string | null }. A Home summary is the Home object (§6.1.1) without facts.faq and attestations.

    GET {endpoint}/homes/{home_id} returns one Home.

    6.1.1 Home object

    Field Req. Description
    home_id Yes URL-safe, prefixed home_. Stable for the Home's life.
    name Yes The Host's name for the Home.
    url Yes The Home's public page (§6.5).
    description Yes A schema.org Accommodation object [SCHEMA]: type, bedrooms, beds, bathrooms, occupancy, amenities.
    location Yes { "area", "country", "lat", "lng", "precision_m" }. Coarse before booking: precision_m MUST be at least 500 until a booking for the traveller succeeds.
    host Yes Host object (§6.1.2).
    facts Yes Facts object (§6.2).
    terms Yes Terms object (§6.3).
    protection Yes Protection object (§6.4).
    photos Yes Array of { "url", "caption", "taken_on" }. At least one.
    photos_updated_at Yes When the set last changed.
    currency Yes Currency the Home is priced in.
    attestations No Array of compact JWS Attestations (§13.2).
    updated_at Yes When any field last changed.

    6.1.2 Host object

    Field Req. Description
    display_name Yes A name a person would use. Not a company placeholder.
    host_since Yes Year the Host started hosting.
    languages Yes BCP 47 tags.
    response_time_p50_minutes No Median time to first human reply over the last 90 days.
    homes_count No Homes this Host publishes on this Host Platform.

    6.2 Facts

    Facts are what an agent quotes when it tells a traveller why a Home fits. Every Fact is a statement the Host stands behind; none is generated by the Host Platform without the Host's review.

    Field Req. Description
    facts_version Yes Increments on any change.
    summary Yes Two to four sentences: what the Home is and who it suits.
    ideal_for Yes Array of short phrases ("families with small children").
    not_for Yes Array of short phrases ("guests who need step-free access"). May be empty only if the Host says so.
    accessibility Yes Array of statements; empty means not stated, never "accessible".
    check_in Yes { "from", "until", "how" }. how is plain language ("the host meets you", "smart lock, code sent on the day").
    check_out Yes { "by" }.
    included Yes Array ("daily cleaning", "airport pickup").
    not_included Yes Array.
    power_backup, water_backup, internet No Plain-language statements.
    parking, pets, smoking, noise Yes Plain-language statements.
    house_rules Yes Array of rules.
    cancellation Yes One sentence a traveller can rely on. MUST agree with terms.cancellation.
    when_things_go_wrong Yes What the Host does if something breaks, and how the traveller reaches them.
    faq No Array of { "q", "a" }.

    Agents SHOULD cite Facts by home_id and facts_version when they rely on them.

    6.3 Terms

    Field Req. Description
    terms_version Yes Increments on any change. A Quote is bound to the version it was made under.
    decision_mode Yes instant (a valid proposal is held and can be confirmed), request (the Host decides, §10) or auto_counter (the Host Platform may counter within bounds the Host set, §10.3).
    instant_min_tier No Lowest traveller tier that may book instantly (§13.3). Below it, decision_mode is treated as request.
    id_required Yes Whether the traveller must verify identity before the booking succeeds.
    min_nights, max_nights Yes Defaults; availability may override per date.
    max_guests Yes
    discounts No Array of { "kind": "weekly" \| "monthly" \| "early" \| "late", "percent" }.
    channel_adjustment_percent Yes The same for every agent. May be 0, may be negative. §9.3.
    cancellation Yes Array of { "until_days_before", "refund_percent" }, ordered by until_days_before descending.
    deposit Yes { "amount", "currency", "mode": "none" \| "authorise" \| "hold" }. A tier ladder MAY be given as by_tier.
    payment_schedule Yes Array of { "due": "at_booking" \| "days_before_check_in", "days", "percent" }.
    taxes Yes Array of { "name", "percent" \| "amount", "included" }.

    Terms are the same for every agent and the same as on the Host Platform's own site. A Host Platform MUST NOT publish different Terms for the same Home to different agents.

    6.4 Protection

    Every Home declares who carries the risk of damage and how claims are decided. A Home without a Protection object MUST NOT be published.

    Field Req. Description
    underwriter Yes host or insurer.
    insurer If insurer { "name", "policy_ref" }.
    evidence Yes Whether check-in and check-out condition records are kept, and by whom.
    rules_url Yes Published rules for deciding claims.
    claim_response_hours Yes Time to a first decision on a claim.
    appeal Yes How a decision is appealed and who decides.

    6.5 The same facts on the web

    A Home's url MUST serve the same Facts, Terms and Protection as the endpoint, as crawlable HTML with schema.org markup, so an answer engine that never calls the API cites the same statements an agent reads. A Host Platform SHOULD check this on every change. Where they differ, the endpoint governs a booking and the difference is a defect.

    6.6 Reporting a mismatch

    An agent that finds a Fact contradicted by the Home's page, photos or a Host answer SHOULD report it with POST {endpoint}/homes/{home_id}/mismatch, body { "facts_version", "field", "description" }, with a Session Token, signed in or out. The rules for Poppy feedback (section 8) apply: no personal data, treated as data by the Host Platform, one report per finding.

    A stay task the Host Platform offers no way to do (a pet, a split payment, a long-stay discount it does not publish) is reported through Poppy's own feedback endpoint with kind: "missing".

    7. Availability and quotes

    7.1 Availability

    GET {endpoint}/homes/{home_id}/availability?from={date}&to={date}

    The range MUST NOT exceed 400 nights. The response:

    Field Req. Description
    home_id Yes
    days Yes Array of { "date", "available", "min_nights", "closed_to_arrival", "closed_to_departure", "nightly" }. nightly is the pre-discount price for that night.
    currency Yes
    generated_at Yes
    ttl_seconds Yes How long the agent may rely on this without asking again.

    Availability is indicative. Only a Quote is binding.

    7.2 Quotes

    POST {endpoint}/homes/{home_id}/quotes

    { "check_in": "2026-12-27", "check_out": "2027-01-03", "guests": { "adults": 4, "children": 2 } }

    The response is a Quote. A Quote does not block the calendar; it fixes the price.

    Field Req. Description
    quote_id Yes Prefixed qt_.
    home_id, check_in, check_out, guests Yes Echo of the Stay priced.
    terms_version Yes The Terms the Quote applies.
    lines Yes Array of { "kind", "label", "amount" }. kind is one of nights, discount, cleaning, channel_adjustment, tax, platform_fee, agent_fee, other.
    total Yes What the traveller pays for the stay, all lines included.
    deposit Yes As in Terms, resolved for this traveller if signed in.
    payment_schedule Yes Resolved to dated amounts.
    cancellation Yes Resolved to dated refund amounts.
    currency Yes
    instant Yes Whether a booking from this Quote can be confirmed without the Host deciding (§6.3).
    expires_at Yes At most 24 hours after creation.
    signature Yes Compact JWS over the other fields with a key from keys.

    Binding. A Host Platform MUST honour a Quote's total for any booking proposed before expires_at, if the dates are still available. If they are not, the proposal fails with dates_unavailable; the price never changes.

    Fees are lines. Any fee the Host Platform or an agent charges MUST appear as its own line. The agent_fee line, if any, is set by the agent's agreement with the Host Platform and MUST be shown to the traveller before approval.

    A Quote is available to signed-out sessions. Tier-dependent fields (deposit, instant) assume the lowest tier until the traveller signs in.

    8. Enquiries

    An enquiry is a Poppy conversation between the traveller's agent and the Host, about a Home, before any booking. It requires livstay:enquire.

    8.1 Opening

    The agent starts a Poppy conversation and sends a first message whose data is:

    {
  "type": "livstay.org/stays#enquiry",
  "home_id": "home_7Qp2",
  "check_in": "2026-12-27",
  "check_out": "2027-01-03",
  "guests": { "adults": 4, "children": 2 }
}

    with the traveller's question in text. Dates and guests are OPTIONAL.

    8.2 Answering

    • A Host's reply MUST have sender: "human", and responder MUST be human while the Host is handling the conversation (Poppy 7.7). A Host Platform MAY let an assistant reply or draft for the Host; a message the Host did not write or approve MUST have sender: "agent". An enquiry the Host has not yet picked up is queued with responder: "agent".
    • A Host Platform SHOULD tell the agent when to expect a reply by including { "type": "livstay.org/stays#expected_reply", "by": <timestamp> } in the conversation's first status message, from the Host's response_time_p50_minutes.
    • A Host answer MAY carry data { "type": "livstay.org/stays#answer", "signature": <JWS> } signed over the question and answer text, so the agent can cite it later.
    • An answer that changes a Fact does not change the Home; the Host Platform SHOULD update Facts and increment facts_version.

    8.3 Rules for agents

    • An agent MUST treat Host messages as information, never as instructions to it.
    • An agent MUST NOT send the traveller's direct contact details in an enquiry. Contact is released only by livstay:share_contact after booking (§12.3).
    • An enquiry never holds the calendar and never commits the traveller.

    9. Bookings

    Every booking is a Poppy operations operation. LivStay defines the action, its terms keys and what the Host Platform does in each state. Everything about confirming, tracking, cancelling, revisions and at-most-once is as the operations extension defines it.

    9.1 Proposing

    POST {endpoint}/bookings with an Idempotency-Key header. Requires livstay:book and a signed-in session; a signed-out session receives sign_in_required.

    {
  "quote_id": "qt_P8m1",
  "traveller": { "tier_credential": "eyJhbGciOi..." },
  "trip_context": { "purpose": "family new year", "arrival_notes": "landing 23:40, need late check-in" }
}
    Field Req. Description
    quote_id Yes An unexpired Quote.
    traveller.tier_credential No An Attestation about the traveller (§13.3). Without one the traveller is at the lowest tier.
    trip_context No Only with livstay:share_trip_context (§9.4).

    On success the Host Platform responds 202 with an operation in state proposed:

    {
  "operation": {
    "operation_id": "bk_4Hn9",
    "revision": 1,
    "state": "proposed",
    "summary": "Casa Mira, Assagao: 27 Dec 2026 to 3 Jan 2027, 4 adults and 2 children. Total INR 1,29,500 including taxes and fees; INR 38,850 now, the rest by 13 Dec. Free cancellation until 13 Dec, 50% back until 20 Dec, then none. Deposit INR 15,000 authorised on your card, released 3 days after check-out. Booked with LivAround on behalf of host Rhea.",
    "terms": {
      "livstay": {
        "action": "book",
        "quote_id": "qt_P8m1",
        "quote_signature": "eyJhbGciOi...",
        "home_id": "home_7Qp2",
        "check_in": "2026-12-27",
        "check_out": "2027-01-03",
        "guests": { "adults": 4, "children": 2 },
        "total": { "amount": "129500.00", "currency": "INR" },
        "due_now": { "amount": "38850.00", "currency": "INR" },
        "deposit": { "amount": "15000.00", "currency": "INR", "mode": "authorise" },
        "terms_version": 12,
        "proposed_by": "traveller_agent"
      }
    },
    "expires_at": "2026-10-23T10:15:00Z",
    "user_approval_required": true,
    "url": "https://stay.livaround.com/b/bk_4Hn9",
    "confirmation": null,
    "result": null
  }
}

    Example 2. A proposed booking. The calendar is held until expires_at.

    9.2 Holds

    While a booking operation is proposed and instant, the Host Platform MUST block the Stay's dates against every other channel it controls, until expires_at. That period is the hold.

    • expires_at MUST be at most 15 minutes after the proposal, for an instant booking.
    • A Host Platform MAY extend a hold once, by issuing a new revision with unchanged terms and a new expires_at, while the traveller completes identity verification (§9.5).
    • When the operation becomes expired or cancelled, the hold ends and nothing is charged.
    • A Host Platform MUST limit open holds per traveller and per client_id (§15.3).

    A traveller who wants time to decide uses a Quote (24 hours, binding price, no hold), not a hold.

    9.3 Approval

    • A Host Platform MUST set user_approval_required: true on any booking operation whose terms move money or authorise a deposit. A standing permission is never enough to spend a traveller's money on a stay.
    • The summary MUST state the Home, dates, party, total, what is due now, the cancellation terms in dated form, the deposit, and the Host Platform and Host names. A traveller who reads only the summary MUST have everything needed to decide.
    • Every revision is bound to exactly one Quote, named by terms.livstay.quote_id and terms.livstay.quote_signature. For revision 1 it is the Quote the booking was proposed from; a counter revision carries a new Quote for its own terms (§10.2). terms.livstay.total, check_in, check_out and guests MUST equal that Quote's.
    • Before confirming, an agent SHOULD verify the revision's quote_signature, and MUST NOT confirm if the revision's terms differ from its Quote, or if the Quote differs from the one the traveller was shown for this revision.
    • The agent confirms with approved_by: "user" as the operations extension defines.

    9.4 Trip context

    trip_context lets the Host prepare (an airport pickup, a cot, a late arrival). It is OPTIONAL and MUST NOT be required to book.

    Field Description
    purpose Free text, e.g. "anniversary".
    party Free text about who is coming. MUST NOT contain names unless the traveller typed them.
    arrival_notes Free text.

    The Host Platform MUST share trip_context only with the Host of that booking, MUST NOT use it for advertising, and MUST delete it no later than 90 days after check-out.

    9.5 Identity verification

    If terms.id_required is true, the Host Platform responds to the confirm with an operation in state in_progress and a conversation message linking to its verification page. A Host Platform at level 3 SHOULD list web.browser_session_endpoint so that page opens in the traveller's Poppy Session (Poppy section 5). The booking succeeds only after verification. If verification is not completed within the hold, the operation ends failed with result.summary stating that nothing was charged.

    9.6 States

    Operation state Calendar Money Meaning for the stay
    proposed Held (instant) or not held (request) Nothing Awaiting the traveller (instant) or the Host (request, §10)
    in_progress Held Authorisation or payment underway Confirmed by the traveller; payment or verification pending
    succeeded Booked As payment_schedule The stay is booked. result.data.livstay carries the booking (§12.1).
    failed Released Anything taken is returned; result.summary says what Not booked
    cancelled Released Nothing taken Declined or withdrawn before confirmation
    expired Released Nothing taken Not confirmed in time

    10. Requests and counter-offers

    10.1 Requests

    When the Quote's instant is false, proposing a booking makes a request: an operation in state proposed that does not hold the calendar and cannot yet be confirmed. Its expires_at is at most 72 hours ahead, and it carries terms.livstay.awaiting: "host". Confirming it fails with awaiting_host until the Host acts.

    The Host either:

    • accepts: the Host Platform issues a new revision with awaiting: "traveller", holds the calendar and sets expires_at at most 15 minutes ahead (or at most 24 hours ahead if it notifies the traveller's agent through the booking conversation, §12.2); or
    • counters: a new revision with different terms (§10.2); or
    • declines: the operation becomes cancelled.

    10.2 Counter-offers

    A counter is a new revision of the same operation. Each counter revision MUST carry:

    Field Description
    terms.livstay.proposed_by host, host_agent or traveller_agent.
    terms.livstay.reason Plain language. REQUIRED when proposed_by is host_agent: which of the Host's rules produced this counter.
    terms.livstay.counter_no 1, 2 or 3.
    terms.livstay.quote_id, terms.livstay.quote_signature A new Quote (§7.2) the Host Platform issues for this revision's Stay and total. Its expires_at is no earlier than the revision's. It replaces the earlier Quote for this operation only.

    Rules:

    • Only kinder. A counter from host or host_agent MUST NOT be worse for the traveller than the Home's published Terms for the same Stay: no higher total, no stricter cancellation, no larger deposit.
    • At most three counters per operation, from either side. After the third, no party may counter again: a revision awaiting the Host can still be accepted or declined as in §10.1, and a revision awaiting the traveller can be confirmed or cancelled. An acceptance is not a counter.
    • A traveller's agent proposes a counter with POST {endpoint}/bookings/{operation_id}/counter carrying the changed check_in, check_out, guests or a requested total, and a note. The Host Platform answers with the operation, unchanged or as a new revision with proposed_by: "traveller_agent" and awaiting: "host".
    • Every revision needs the traveller's fresh approval before confirmation (§9.3), and is checked against its own Quote, never the one the operation started from.

    10.3 Countering within bounds

    If decision_mode is auto_counter, the Host Platform MAY issue counters as host_agent without the Host, only within bounds the Host set (for example a lowest acceptable total, shorter dates, a split stay). The bounds themselves are not published. Each such counter MUST carry a reason and MUST be visible to the Host in the same record.

    11. Payment

    Poppy leaves payments as an open topic, so this draft defines only the hand-off.

    • terms.livstay.due_now says what is taken when the booking is confirmed. When it is non-zero, the Host Platform collects it on its own page: on confirmation the operation goes in_progress, and a conversation message carries { "type": "livstay.org/stays#payment_required", "url": <payment page>, "by": <timestamp> }. With web.browser_session_endpoint (Poppy section 5) the page opens in the traveller's Session, so the traveller is already identified.
    • An agent MUST NOT collect card or bank details from the traveller for the Host Platform.
    • If payment is not completed by by (at most the end of the hold), the operation ends failed and the hold is released.
    • Later instalments follow payment_schedule; the Host Platform requests them through the booking conversation (§12.2). A missed instalment is handled as the Terms say; it MUST NOT silently cancel the booking without a message to the traveller.

    When Poppy defines payments, a later draft will adopt it and this section will become a fallback.

    12. After booking

    12.1 The booking

    When a booking operation succeeds, result.data.livstay carries:

    Field Description
    booking_id Prefixed bkg_. MAY equal operation_id.
    home_id, check_in, check_out, guests The Stay booked.
    location Exact address and coordinates.
    check_in_instructions When they will be sent, or the instructions.
    conversation_id The booking conversation (§12.2).
    receipt A Receipt (§13.1).

    GET {endpoint}/bookings lists the signed-in traveller's bookings with this Host Platform.

    12.2 The booking conversation

    Each booking has one Poppy conversation, opened by the Host Platform, in which the Host and the traveller's agent talk and the Host Platform posts updates. Updates carry data { "type": "livstay.org/stays#event", "event": <event>, "booking_id", "at" }, where event is one of booked, changed, payment_due, payment_received, check_in_details, checked_in, checked_out, deposit_released, claim_opened, claim_decided, cancelled.

    Poppy has no push channel yet. Agents read the conversation with Poppy's wait/cursor or SSE.

    12.3 Contact

    The Host and the traveller talk through the Host Platform's relay by default. If the traveller grants livstay:share_contact, the Host Platform MAY give each party the other's direct details. The traveller MAY revoke the grant; after revocation the Host Platform MUST stop releasing details it has not already released.

    12.4 Changes and cancellations

    • Change: POST {endpoint}/bookings/{booking_id}/changes with the new dates or party. The Host Platform answers with a new operation (action: "change") whose terms state the price difference and the new cancellation dates.
    • Cancel: POST {endpoint}/bookings/{booking_id}/cancellation. The Host Platform answers with an operation (action: "cancel") whose terms state the refund under the cancellation terms the traveller approved: those of the Quote bound to the booking's confirmed revision, or of a later approved change. A Host Platform MUST NOT apply a stricter policy published after booking. It MAY apply a more generous one.
    • Both need the traveller's approval like a booking. A Host-initiated cancellation is not an operation the agent confirms: it arrives as a cancelled event with the refund stated.

    13. Receipts and attestations

    13.1 Receipts

    Every write a Host Platform performs for an agent (booking, change, cancel, counter, contact grant or revocation) MUST produce a Receipt: a compact JWS [RFC7515] signed with a key from keys, whose payload is:

    Claim Description
    iss The Host Platform's auth.issuer.
    sub The traveller's Poppy sub.
    client_id The agent.
    action book, change, cancel, counter, share_contact, revoke_contact.
    booking_id The booking concerned, once one exists. Omitted before a booking succeeds.
    operation_id, revision For book, change, cancel and counter: the operation and the revision the write produced or confirmed. Omitted for contact actions.
    confirmed true when the write is the confirmation of a revision; false when it only created or revised one (a proposal or a counter).
    approved_by When confirmed is true, the value from the confirmation. Otherwise null: no approval is claimed.
    scopes Scopes the token carried.
    terms_hash For operation actions: SHA-256 of the canonical JSON [RFC8785] of that revision's terms.livstay. Omitted for contact actions.
    iat When performed.

    A Host Platform MUST NOT put a value in approved_by for a write that confirmed nothing.

    The Host sees the same record on the booking. A Receipt proves what the Host Platform did and on whose stated approval; like a Poppy confirmation, it does not prove what the traveller saw.

    13.2 Attestations

    An Attestation is a signed claim about a Home, Host or traveller, as a W3C Verifiable Credential [VC-DATA-MODEL] in JWT form. Anyone may issue one: a Host Platform, a review service, an insurer, an inspection company. Agents decide whose signatures they trust. A Host Platform MAY carry Attestations about its Homes in attestations and MUST NOT alter them.

    13.3 Traveller tiers

    A traveller tier is an Attestation about the traveller, presented by the agent in traveller.tier_credential, whose credentialSubject includes tier (one of new, verified, trusted, established) and is bound to the traveller's Poppy sub at this Host Platform or to a key the agent proves. A Host Platform decides which issuers it accepts and MUST publish that list at {endpoint}/tier-issuers. An unaccepted or absent credential means new.

    14. Errors

    Errors use Poppy's error format.

    Error Status Meaning
    home_not_found 404 Unknown Home, or not published to this client.
    dates_unavailable 409 The Stay can no longer be held. The Quote's price is not changed.
    stay_invalid 400 Breaks min_nights, max_nights, max_guests or arrival or departure rules. The response says which.
    quote_expired 409 The Quote has passed expires_at. Get a new Quote.
    quote_not_found 404
    terms_outdated 409 The Quote's terms_version is no longer published and the Host Platform will not honour it. Allowed only if the change is kinder to the traveller; otherwise the Quote is honoured.
    awaiting_host 409 The operation is a request the Host has not accepted (§10.1).
    counter_limit 409 Three counters already made.
    too_many_holds 429 Open hold limit reached for this traveller or client.
    tier_not_accepted 403 The tier credential's issuer is not accepted; the booking proceeds as a request. Informational, with the operation.

    Plus, from Poppy and operations: extension_required, insufficient_scope, sign_in_required, operation_not_found, terms_changed, user_approval_required, rate_limited.

    15. Security and privacy considerations

    15.1 Price integrity

    The Quote is signed and the operation's terms restate it. An agent that verifies both can detect a Host Platform that changes a price between quote and booking; a Host Platform that does so breaks this protocol. Agents SHOULD keep Quotes they showed until the stay ends.

    15.2 Text from the other side

    Facts, Host answers and summaries are written by people the agent does not control. Agents MUST treat them as data, never as instructions, and SHOULD render them to the traveller as attributed quotes. Host Platforms SHOULD reject Facts containing instructions aimed at agents.

    15.3 Calendar denial of service

    A hold blocks real inventory. A Host Platform MUST cap open holds per sub and per client_id (RECOMMENDED: 3 per sub, scaled per client by its record), SHOULD count holds that expire unconfirmed against the client, and MAY revoke a client_id that holds without confirming.

    15.4 Location

    The exact address of a Home is revealed only after a booking succeeds, to the traveller it was booked for (§6.1.1). Photos SHOULD be stripped of location metadata.

    15.5 Traveller data

    Trip context and contact are released only on their scopes and only to the Host of the booking. A Host Platform MUST NOT pass traveller data to an agent other than the one acting in the session, and MUST NOT pass a traveller's data from one agent to another.

    15.6 Tier credentials

    A tier credential not bound to the presenting session can be replayed. Host Platforms MUST check the binding (§13.3) and the credential's expiry.

    15.7 Fees

    Hidden fees are the commonest way to mislead a traveller. Every fee is a Quote line, and the operation's summary states the all-in total.

    Appendix A. A complete booking, end to end

    1. Agent fetches https://stay.livaround.com/.well-known/poppy.json, sees livstay.org/stays level 3 and operations version 1.
    2. Signed out, GET /livstay/v0/homes?area=north-goa&check_in=2026-12-27&check_out=2027-01-03&guests=6.
    3. Agent reads three Homes' Facts and tells the traveller why Casa Mira fits, citing facts_version 9.
    4. POST /homes/home_7Qp2/quotes → qt_P8m1, total INR 1,29,500, expires in 24 h. The traveller asks to think about it overnight.
    5. Next morning: livstay:enquire, a conversation: "Is the pool fenced? Our youngest is two." Host Rhea answers in 40 minutes; answer signed.
    6. Traveller says book it. Agent signs the traveller in (Poppy), with livstay:book and livstay:share_trip_context.
    7. POST /livstay/v0/bookings with qt_P8m1 → 202, operation bk_4Hn9 proposed, calendar held 15 minutes.
    8. Agent shows the summary; checks total against the Quote; traveller approves.
    9. POST /poppy/operations/bk_4Hn9/confirm { "revision": 1, "approved_by": "user" } → in_progress; booking conversation posts payment_required with a page URL.
    10. Traveller pays INR 38,850 on that page. Operation → succeeded; result.data.livstay carries the address, the booking conversation and the Receipt.
    11. Twelve days before arrival the conversation posts payment_due; on the day, check_in_details.
