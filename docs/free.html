Why now

Travellers have started asking an assistant where to stay.

An assistant can only book what it can read and price. Today that leaves most independent homes out of the answer.

The maths

The guest pays less. You keep more.

Around 15% of what a guest pays on Airbnb goes to Airbnb. A LivStay booking costs 5%, plus the card processing fee. The difference is yours to share with the guest.

The fine print, up front

Four things to know before you start.

We earn only when you do

The 5% comes out of a booking's payment when one arrives through LivStay or your booking page. No setup fee, no monthly fee, and no fee on anything you earn on Airbnb.

Guests pay into your own Razorpay account

Razorpay takes our 5% and its own processing fee out of the payment, and settles the rest to your bank on its normal schedule. There is no bill to pay later. A new Razorpay account needs their usual checks before it can take money.

Your calendar is yours to keep true

We sync with Airbnb about every half hour, and a direct booking blocks your dates at once. If the same dates sell twice in between, the direct guest is refunded from your account.

India first

At launch, LivStay Free takes payments in rupees through Razorpay, for homes in India. Other countries will follow.

Questions hosts ask

Before you paste the link

Do I have to leave Airbnb?

No. Your listing, your reviews and your Airbnb bookings stay as they are. LivStay Free adds more ways to get booked and keeps the two calendars in step.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no subscription, no card to enter and no limit on homes. When a booking arrives through LivStay or your booking page, 5% comes out of that payment. You are never sent a bill.

How does an AI assistant book my home?

It reads your home's facts, rules and cancellation terms through LivStay, checks your dates and gets a signed price. The traveller then pays on your booking page. Nothing is booked without them paying.

What happens to my reviews?

Your Airbnb rating and review count come across with the listing and show on your booking page.

When do I get paid?

The guest pays into your own Razorpay account at booking. Razorpay takes out our 5% and its processing fee, and settles the rest to your bank on its normal schedule.

Do I need a Razorpay account?

Yes, your own. If you have one, connecting it takes a minute. If not, you can open one during setup; Razorpay asks for your PAN and bank details and checks them before your first payment.

Can a guest cancel?

You choose one of three policies, flexible, moderate or strict, and the guest sees it before they pay. Refunds follow the policy you picked.

What is not included?

The tools for running a stay: guest messaging, cleaning and staff, smart locks, pricing tools and your own domain. Those are in LivAround Pro, and you can move up at any time.
